Summary Of Key Points

  • What personal information do we process? When you visit, use, or navigate our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the features you use (such as creating a profile, logging album reviews, rating tracks, and interacting with friends).

  • What are your rights? Depending on your geographical location, you have rights regarding access, portability, correction, and deletion of your data.
  • How do you exercise your rights? Contact us at chordsmusicapp@gmail.com or use the in-app account deletion tool in Profile Settings.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us. We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, set up a profile, log albums, publish reviews, rate songs, send friend requests, or contact us.

  • Account Identifiers: Names, email addresses, and chosen unique usernames.
  • Authentication Details: Passwords (securely hashed via Firebase Authentication) or third-party federated login tokens (Google Sign-In).
  • User-Generated Content & Social Data: Profile pictures/avatars (uploaded image data or Base64 image strings), logged album titles, artist names, music ratings, written reviews, track notes, and emoji reactions.
  • Social Graph & Trust & Safety Records: Friend lists, pending friend requests, lists of blocked user identifiers, and content moderation/abuse reports submitted by or filed against you. Sensitive Information We do not process sensitive personal information (such as racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, health data, or political opinions). Application Data If you use our mobile application, we also may collect the following information if you choose to grant permission:
  • Mobile Device Data: We automatically collect device information such as your mobile device model, manufacturer, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Photos & Media Library: If you choose to upload a custom profile picture, we request read access to your photo library to select and upload the image.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Short: We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, enforce our community standards, and comply with law. We process your personal information for purposes including:

  • Account & Service Management: Facilitating account registration, authentication, and profile maintenance.
  • Social & Music Logging Experience: Rendering your album reviews, track ratings, track notes, friend activity feeds, and profile views.
  • Community Safety, Moderation, and UGC Compliance: Processing user reports, investigating violations of our Terms of Use, enforcing our zero-tolerance policy against objectionable content or harassment, and managing user-initiated blocks.
  • Communication & Support: Responding to inquiries, sending email verification links, password reset instructions, and administrative updates.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our Services against spam, bot activity, abuse, and security incidents.

  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent (e.g., photo library access).
  • Performance of a Contract: Fulfilling our commitment to provide you with the music rating and community features.

  • Legal Obligations: Complying with regulatory requests or legal processes. Canada We rely on express or implied consent under applicable Canadian privacy legislation, except where permitted or required by law to proceed without consent (such as legal investigations or fraud prevention).

4. When And With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

In Short: We share information with service providers that help operate our app, and publicly with other users as part of our social features.

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database Providers: Google Cloud Platform, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore for database storage, real-time sync, and security.
  • Music API Services: Spotify API to retrieve album artwork, release metadata, and track listings.
  • Other Users: Content you publicly submit (including your username, profile photo, logged albums, ratings, track notes, reviews, and emoji reactions) will be visible to your friends and other users on the platform.
  • Safety & Law Enforcement: When required by law or to protect user safety and investigate severe harassment or abusive behavior.

5. How Do We Handle Your Social Logins?

In Short: If you choose to log in using Google, we access basic identity details provided by Google. When you authenticate through Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, full name, and authentication token to establish your account. We do not access external contacts, Google Drive files, or any unrelated account data.

6. Is Your Information Transferred Internationally?

In Short: We may store and process your information in the United States. Our backend infrastructure and Firebase Firestore databases are located in the United States. If you access our Services from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed within the United States. We implement standard contractual safeguards to ensure data protection.

7. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

In Short: We keep your information as long as your account is active, or as needed for safety and moderation records.

  • Account Data & Music Reviews: Retained for the lifetime of your account. If you tap Delete Account in your Profile Settings, all of your profile details, catalog albums, ratings, reviews, and notes are permanently deleted from our active databases.
  • Safety, Block, and Abuse Records: Blocked user records are stored until unblocked. Submitted abuse reports and moderation logs are retained for a limited operational period to ensure security and prevent banned users from circumventing enforcement.

8. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: We use technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. We protect your information using secure HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, Firebase security rules, authenticated access barriers, and encrypted database storage. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely impenetrable.

9. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

In Short: We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 years of age. Our Services are intended for individuals 18 years of age and older. By using the Services, you confirm that you meet this age requirement. If we discover that personal data from an individual under 18 has been collected without parental consent, we will deactivate the account and delete the data. If you believe we have data on a minor, email us at chordsmusicapp@gmail.com.

10. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or certain US states), you have the right to:

  • Request access to and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (available directly inside the app via Profile Settings > Delete Account).
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • Withdraw previously given consent. To submit a request, email chordsmusicapp@gmail.com.

11. Controls For Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems offer a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") setting. Because there is no uniform industry standard for recognizing DNT signals, our Services do not currently respond to automated DNT browser signals.
