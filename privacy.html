Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Chords: Rate & Review Music ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our mobile application, Chords: Rate & Review Music (the "App"), and any other services that link to this Privacy Notice (collectively, the "Services"). Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions, please contact us at chordsmusicapp@gmail.com.

Summary Of Key Points

What personal information do we process? We process the account information you give us directly (such as your email address and username), the content you create in the App (ratings, reviews, your collection and wantlist), your social connections within the App (friends and friend requests), and limited information from the third-party services you choose to connect, such as Spotify.

Do we process any sensitive personal information? We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as health data, racial or ethnic origin, or religious beliefs. Any such information included in a review or profile is added voluntarily by you and is visible to other users.

Do we collect information from third parties? Yes. If you connect a Spotify account, we receive limited profile and listening information from Spotify as described below. If you use the barcode scanner, we send the scanned barcode (not your personal information) to Discogs to look up catalog details. If you grant access to your iPhone's Apple Music library, we read certain local listening statistics on your device to power recommendations; this information is not uploaded to our servers.

How do we process your information? We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, to let you connect and share with friends, to communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We also process your information for other purposes with your consent. We process your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.

In what situations and with which parties do we share personal information? We may share information with service providers who help us run the Services (such as Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Spotify, and Discogs), with other users as part of the App's social and community features, and in connection with a business transfer, as described in the "WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION" section below.

How do we keep your information safe? We use appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including industry-standard authentication and encrypted data transmission, to protect your personal information. No electronic transmission or storage system is 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that unauthorized third parties will never be able to defeat those measures.

What are your rights? Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can delete your account and most associated data at any time directly within the App.

How do you exercise your rights? The easiest way is to use the in-app account deletion tool described below, or to contact us at chordsmusicapp@gmail.com. We will respond to any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us:

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register for the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contact us.

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (if you sign up with email), or basic profile information from Apple or Google if you sign up with "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google" (typically your name and email address, as shared by Apple or Google). You also choose a unique username, which is public.

Profile information. You may optionally add a profile picture. Before a profile picture is uploaded, it is scanned on your device to check that it does not contain explicit content; this scan happens entirely on your device and the image itself is not sent anywhere for that check. If the image passes this check, it is stored, as image data, associated with your account.

Content you create. We collect the content you create in the App, including star ratings, written reviews, track-level notes, "favorite" flags, vinyl/CD variant and catalog-number notes you add to your own collection, and your wantlist. Reviews, ratings, and your activity in the App's community feed are visible to other users as described in our Terms of Service.

Social information. If you add friends within the App, we store the connection between your account and theirs (friend requests sent, accepted, and your friends list) so that we can show you each other's public activity.

Communications. If you contact us directly (for example, by email), we may receive additional information about you, such as the contents of your message and any information you choose to provide.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Chords Premium, your purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status (active or not) so that premium features can be unlocked on your account.

Information automatically collected:

Some information — such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and app-usage information — is collected automatically by the third-party services we use (described in Section 4 and Section 6 below), such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob, when you use the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, device and browser characteristics, operating system, and other technical information.

Information from the camera:

The App uses your device's camera only to scan vinyl and CD barcodes so you can quickly add an item to your collection. The camera feed is processed on your device to detect a barcode; we do not save photos or video from this feature, and the camera is never accessed for any other purpose.

Information from your photo library:

If you choose to set a profile picture, the App accesses your photo library only for the single photo you select, using Apple's limited picker, which does not give the App access to your full photo library.

Push notification token:

If you allow notifications, we store a device token (provided by Apple and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) linked to your account so our server can send you notifications about things like friend requests and activity on your reviews. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App's settings or in your device's Settings app.

5. Third-Party Music Services & Your Listening Data

Spotify:

If you choose to connect your Spotify account, you will be asked by Spotify to approve specific permissions ("scopes"). We request the following scopes: your Spotify email address and basic profile information, your recently played tracks, and your top tracks and artists. We use this information only to power your in-app recommendations, collection, and discovery feeds, and we do not sell this information. You can disconnect your Spotify account at any time in your profile settings, and you can also manage or revoke Chords' access directly from your Spotify account settings at spotify.com.

Apple Music:

If you grant permission, the App reads certain listening statistics already stored in your iPhone's local Apple Music library — such as play counts and last-played dates for albums in your library — to power the App's Discover feed. This information is read and processed entirely on your device. We do not upload your Apple Music listening history to our servers.

Discogs:

The barcode scanner sends only the scanned barcode number to Discogs' public catalog API to retrieve release information (artwork, track listing, pressing details). No account or personal information is sent to Discogs through this feature.

Chords is an independent music-journaling platform and is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with Spotify AB, Apple Inc., Google LLC, or Discogs.
